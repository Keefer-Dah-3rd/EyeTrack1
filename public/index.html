Eye tracker

Records a test session on any page: which areas people look at and what they read using your webcam, plus exact mouse, clicks and scrolling.

  • Sit about an arm's length from the screen, with light in front of you rather than behind.
  • Keep your head still from calibration onward. Leaning against a headrest or resting your elbows helps.
  • Set the browser window to the size you'll test at. Moving or resizing it later means recalibrating.
  • Video is processed in this browser and is never uploaded.
